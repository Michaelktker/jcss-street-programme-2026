          06

          Festival pass & redemption programme

          The mechanic that turns footfall into merchant revenue: a physical pass, a capped free-drink redemption, and a $5 voucher that stays spendable after the event.

          Festival Pass

          About 1,000 passes are handed by participating merchants to their loyal customers, carrying perks such as free coffee, free beer and $5 vouchers. These are passes, not tickets — entry to the event itself is free. Distributed through merchants and on social media in the weeks before the event.

          Figures differ between documents: the 2 Sep minutes proposed 1,500 passes (over-issued from 1,000 to cover a ~0.6–0.7 redemption rate). Final numbers sit with INVADE.

          On the day: collect a wrist tag at the redemption counter, then redeem pass perks with programme partners. Entry itself is free for the general public.

          • Pass holders follow the pass-holder route — arrival, registration, redemption counter, challenges, then explore Joo Chiat.
          • Non-pass visitors get the free-entry route — enjoy the programme and explore.
          • Children under 6 enter free with adult supervision.

          Coffee & beer redemption

          Participating cafés and bars welcome visitors with a complimentary signature coffee or a half-pint / craft beer. Redemptions are capped per merchant and first-come, first-served.

          ChannelAllocationKJCBA reimburses
          Cafés150 coffees each$4.00 per coffee redeemed
          Bars — half-pint120 half-pints each$6.50 per half-pint redeemed
          Bars — craft bottles48 craft beers (KJCBA-provided)Redeemed before half-pints

          Merchants keep the collected vouchers and hand them to KJCBA within 7 days of the event; reimbursement follows the actual number redeemed, never above the allocated ceiling.

          $5 event voucher

          A separate physical voucher won through the on-site games and challenges, and a mechanic built to support the hawker ecosystem: redeemable at Dunman Food Centre and settled dollar-for-dollar by INVADE, with no revenue share. The 2 Sep minutes put its life at 30 days after the event; it is not exchangeable for cash.

          Sources conflict: the INVADE deck’s voucher terms say it is valid for one-time use during the event period only. Not yet reconciled — see Open items.

              How it settles

              The $5 voucher mechanic was built to support the hawker ecosystem: vouchers presented at these stalls are settled dollar-for-dollar by INVADE, with no revenue share and no TTO — so a redeemed voucher costs the stall nothing.

              Look for the participating-stall decal. Vouchers are won by taking part in the challenges on the day, and are not exchangeable for cash.

              Held back on purpose: stall contact persons, mobile numbers, licence numbers and the participation-fee status are not published here — they stay in KJCBA's own records.
