05

Festival pass & redemption programme

The mechanic that turns footfall into merchant revenue: a physical pass, a capped free-drink redemption, and a $5 voucher that stays spendable after the event.

Festival Pass

About 1,500 passes are issued (over-issued from 1,000 to cover a ~0.6–0.7 redemption rate) and distributed by participating merchants and on social media in the weeks before the event.

On the day: collect a wrist tag at the redemption counter, then redeem pass perks with programme partners. Entry itself is free for the general public.

  • Pass holders follow the pass-holder route — arrival, registration, redemption counter, challenges, then explore Joo Chiat.
  • Non-pass visitors get the free-entry route — enjoy the programme and explore.
  • Children under 6 enter free with adult supervision.

Coffee & beer redemption

Participating cafés and bars welcome visitors with a complimentary signature coffee or a half-pint / craft beer. Redemptions are capped per merchant and first-come, first-served.

ChannelAllocationKJCBA reimburses
Cafés150 coffees each$4.00 per coffee redeemed
Bars — half-pint120 half-pints each$6.50 per half-pint redeemed
Bars — craft bottles48 craft beers (KJCBA-provided)Redeemed before half-pints

Merchants keep the collected vouchers and hand them to KJCBA within 7 days of the event; reimbursement follows the actual number redeemed, never above the allocated ceiling.

$5 event voucher

A separate physical voucher won through the on-site games and challenges. The 2 Sep minutes put it at 30 days after the event, spendable at onboarded merchants (look for the decal) and not exchangeable for cash.

Sources conflict: the INVADE deck\u2019s voucher terms say it is valid for one-time use during the event period only. Not yet reconciled \u2014 see Open items.
